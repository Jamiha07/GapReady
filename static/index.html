About GapReady

GapReady helps students walk into internship and entry-level interviews already knowing where they'll be tested. Upload your resume, paste the job description you're applying for, and GapReady turns the space between the two into a practice session built just for you.

Finds your gaps
Compares your resume against the job description and pinpoints the exact skills the role asks for that your resume doesn't yet show.
Questions that target them
Each gap becomes an interview question, asked at the difficulty the role actually demands — so nothing catches you off guard on the day.
Grounded in your projects
Extra questions built around the projects on your own resume, the same way a real interviewer would dig into them.
Behavioral rounds covered
Core questions on teamwork, communication, and problem-solving are always included.
Hints and follow-ups
Every question comes with a short hint on what a strong answer covers, plus the follow-up an interviewer would likely ask next.
Practice recall first
Full sample answers appear only when you ask for them, so you try answering yourself before peeking.
Take it with you
Export your full prep sheet as a PDF or a plain text file.
